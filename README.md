## 🌟 Overview

The **AI Resume Analyzer** is a smart web application that evaluates your resume against modern **ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems)**.  
It helps job seekers understand how well their resume aligns with a specific **job role** by providing:
- An **ATS compatibility score**
- A **category-wise breakdown**
- And **AI-powered suggestions** for improvement

Whether you’re applying for a **Data Analyst**, **AI/ML Engineer**, or **Software Developer** role — this tool ensures your resume passes the first screening confidently.

---

## 🧠 Features

✅ **AI-Powered ATS Scoring** – Get a percentage score that reflects how optimized your resume is.  
✅ **Detailed Category Breakdown** – Understand your score distribution across Keywords, Skills, Structure & Formatting.  
✅ **Smart Suggestions** – Personalized feedback to improve your resume’s job relevance.  
✅ **Modern UI** – Sleek black-and-green design with circular gauges and interactive charts.  
✅ **Role-Based Analysis** – Tailored feedback for your selected job role.
